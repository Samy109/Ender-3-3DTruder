| Feature | As modelled |
|---|---|
| Body | Ø24.7 × 40, excluding stub |

| Wrench flats | 21.7 A/F, 20–26 from the shoulder |
| Heater hole | Ø6.3 through, perpendicular to the axis, 6.5 off-center, 7 from the shoulder; 45° teardrop roof |
| Thermistor hole | Ø3.3 × 16 deep, parallel to the heater hole, 6.5 off-center on the opposite side, 15 from the shoulder; 45° teardrop roof |
| Set screws | M5×0.8 internal threads, printed, from the shoulder face into the heater and thermistor holes |

**Notes**
1. Print axis vertical, top (M12) face on the plate, stub up. The teardrop roofs and the cone floor are there so no internal supports are needed.
2. Every fit already allows for ±0.3. The threads are offset in the model: the M6 is 0.3 under size and the M12 and M5s are 0.3 over. Holes the cartridges slide into are 0.3 over, and the flats and the clamp round are 0.3 under. Print the model as it is; don't correct these sizes back to nominal.
3. Keep the bore, threads and cross holes clear of powder.

**Customer checks on arrival**
- [ ] The M6 stub screws into the Super Volcano block by hand
- [ ] The barrel's M12×1.0 thread screws into the top by hand
- [ ] A 6 mm cartridge slides into the heater hole and a 3 mm thermistor into its hole
- [ ] M5 set screws run in by hand or with a hex key
- [ ] A 22 mm wrench fits the flats
- [ ] Clear sight line through the bore
